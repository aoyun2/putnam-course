# Course profile: Olympiad and Putnam Problem Solving

- **Student:** Aaron Xiong (self-study; graded by Claude as instructor).
- **Dates:** 23 November 2026 to 21 August 2027 (37 weeks; finals weeks 14–20 Dec 2026 and 10–16 May 2027 have no work). Afterwards, weekly past-problem sets until the Putnam in December 2027.
- **Syllabus:** `src/syllabus.tex` (version of 18 Nov 2026). The schedule table there is the topic sequence.
- **Goal:** a top-100 finish on the Putnam.
- **Sources:** Gelca and Andreescu, *Putnam and Beyond* (main text); Zeitz, *The Art and Craft of Problem Solving*; Engel, *Problem-Solving Strategies*; for each area the books listed in the syllabus; past Putnam, USAMO, IMO and IMO Shortlist papers. The books are Aaron's private copies on the course's private artifact; they are never committed to this repository.
- **Prerequisites:** calculus through multivariable calculus; proofs at the level of a first proof-based course.
- **Assessment:** weekly problem sets (due Sunday 11:59 pm, Pacific), Monday quizzes (60 minutes, 3 problems, Putnam scale), unit tests and monthly mock contests (3 hours, 6 problems), final mock Putnam. Weights: problem sets 35, quizzes 20, unit tests and mock contests 30, final 15.
- **Submission:** Google Classroom, through the bridge on the `classroom` branch.
- **Publication restrictions (handout standard 5b):**
  - `publish_solutions`: extra practice — true, published with the handout (it is not graded, so the syllabus rule on reading solutions does not apply). Graded problems (problem sets, quizzes, tests, mock contests) — only after Aaron's work on them has been graded.   - Quizzes, tests and mock contests stay on the private artifact until they are given.
  - No books, scans or long excerpts on `main`.
